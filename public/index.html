Fire Safety FAQ — Dubai & UAE

What makes IgniShield the best fire and safety company in Dubai?

IgniShield is Dubai's only DCD A+ Grade fire company with a 100% DCD inspection pass rate, under-2-hour emergency response SLA, ISO 9001 certification, and over 500 successfully completed fire protection projects in Dubai spanning commercial, industrial, hospitality, and residential sectors.

Is a fire AMC annual maintenance contract mandatory in Dubai?

Yes. Dubai Civil Defence mandates that all buildings maintain an active fire AMC contract in Dubai with a licensed fire fighting company. Buildings without a valid annual maintenance contract face fines, failed Civil Defence inspections, and potential trade licence suspension or cancellation.

What is an Istifa fire safety certificate and who needs it in Dubai?

The Istifa certificate is the official fire safety compliance certificate issued by Dubai Civil Defence after a successful building inspection. All commercial, residential, and industrial properties in Dubai are legally required to hold and annually renew the Istifa — obtainable only through a DCD-approved fire company in Dubai.

How long does fire system installation take in Dubai?

A standard office or retail fit-out fire alarm installation in Dubai typically takes 2–4 weeks from DCD approval to Istifa certification. Larger industrial or commercial fire protection projects in Dubai — including sprinklers, hydrants, and suppression systems — range from 6–16 weeks depending on scope, building area, and DCD processing timelines.
